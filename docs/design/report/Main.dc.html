How scores are computed

Each product's score (0 to 100) combines threat signals for its CVEs (CVSS, EPSS, CISA KEV listing, public exploit availability), the context of the affected assets (internet exposure, Defender exposure level, device value, criticality tags) and the number of affected devices. Weights live in scoring.yaml.

Grouping

Findings are grouped by software product so one row maps to one patch action. Only the three CVEs contributing most to a product's score are shown, and only its most critical assets. The full CVE and device lists are in findings.json.
